THE PROBLEM

People don't always do what businesses expect.

  • Customers abandon products they said they wanted.
  • Users stop engaging with experiences they initially loved.
  • Employees resist systems designed to make their work easier.
  • Patients struggle to follow through with products built to help them.

The problem is rarely just the product.

It is often the behavior around it.

WHAT WE SOLVE

The questions at the center of our work.

01

ADOPTION

Why aren't people starting?

First use is harder than it looks. We identify what prevents people from taking the first step — and what makes it more likely.

02

ENGAGEMENT

Why aren't they continuing?

Initial interest rarely becomes habit on its own. We find what breaks the cycle — and what builds it.

03

CONVERSION

Why aren't they choosing?

People understand the offer but don't act. We diagnose the moment of hesitation and design for what comes next.

04

RETENTION

Why aren't they returning?

Value alone doesn't keep people. We examine what reduces the motivation to return — and what restores it.

05

DECISIONS

Why are choices diverging from expectations?

Customers and employees often make decisions that seem irrational. We find the underlying logic — and work with it, not against it.

06

CHANGE

Why aren't new behaviors taking hold?

Behavioral change is hard by design. We understand why — and build pathways that make the desired behavior easier, clearer, and more sustainable.

OUR APPROACH

How we find what's really going on — and what to do about it.

01

UNDERSTAND

We start by mapping what people are actually doing — not what they are expected to do. Observation before assumption.

02

DIAGNOSE

We identify the behavioral barriers: cognitive load, poor timing, unclear value, social friction, broken habits.

03

DESIGN

We develop targeted interventions grounded in behavioral evidence — from small friction reductions to full journey redesigns.

04

EXPERIMENT

We test which intervention actually moves behavior in the real world. Rigorous where it matters, pragmatic where it doesn't.

05

MEASURE

We track whether behavior changed — and whether the business outcome changed with it. Insight without outcome is incomplete.

— AN ILLUSTRATIVE EXAMPLE —

OBSERVATION

Users sign up but fail to return after day one. Activation is weak. 30-day retention is worse.

BEHAVIORAL QUESTION

What prevents the behavior from becoming routine? Is it friction, unclear reward, poor timing, or missing progress feedback?

INTERVENTION

Reduce cognitive load at first use. Add one clear, immediate win. Introduce well-timed, contextual follow-ups.

MEASURE

A/B test the redesigned onboarding. Track activation rate and 30-day return behavior as primary outcomes.
